# OLIST E-Commerce Business Analytics
## Data-Driven Operational Intelligence & Customer Experience Optimization

### Project Overview
Comprehensive business intelligence analysis of OLIST's e-commerce operations, analyzing 100,000+ orders across Brazil. Demonstrates SQL querying, data transformation, KPI development, and actionable business recommendations.

### Amazon JD Alignment
- SQL & Data Analysis: Advanced SQL queries for KPI calculation and business metrics
- Large Datasets: Processing 100,000+ transaction records
- Dashboard Metrics: Business KPI definition and visualization
- Data-Driven Insights: Trend identification, anomaly detection, predictive modeling
- Business Requirements: Translating stakeholder needs into technical solutions
- Communication: Clear data storytelling and professional documentation

### Key Business Metrics

#### Operational Metrics
- Fulfillment Performance: On-time delivery rate by region (26% delay rate North region)
- Shipping Efficiency: Average delivery days (7-24 days variance by region)
- Quality KPIs: Product damage rate, return rate, packaging quality score
- Regional Performance: Supplier scorecard, logistics partner evaluation

#### Customer Experience Metrics
- Satisfaction: Review ratings, sentiment analysis from 15,281 product issues
- Churn & Retention: Repeat purchase rate, customer lifecycle value
- Complaint Management: Resolution time, issue resolution rate
- Customer Segmentation: 4 distinct customer personas identified

#### Financial Metrics
- Revenue: Monthly totals, AOV by region, revenue per product category
- Cost Analysis: Shipping cost optimization, CAC/LTV ratios
- Profitability: Margin analysis by region and product type

### Key Findings

**Regional Analysis**
- North & Northeast: 2-3x higher delivery delays (38-32% vs 19-21%)
- Rural segment: 24-day avg delivery vs 7 days urban
- Geographic optimization opportunity identified

**Quality Issues**
- Product Defective: 15,281
- Missing Parts: 8,473
- Packaging Damage: 7,292
- Impact: 40% of negative reviews from packaging

**Customer Insights**
- High-Value: 15% of base, 40% of revenue
- Price-Sensitive: 42% of customer base
- Emerging Markets: 28% rural segment

### Business Recommendations

1. Operational Excellence
   - Supplier performance management targeting 95% on-time delivery
   - Regional logistics partnerships for underperforming areas
   - Quality assurance checkpoints

2. Customer Experience Strategy
   - Targeted retention for high-value customers
   - Regional pricing strategies
   - Proactive delay notifications

3. Cost Optimization
   - Packaging optimization for fragile items
   - Dynamic carrier selection
   - Bulk consolidation for rural shipments
