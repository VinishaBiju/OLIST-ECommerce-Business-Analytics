# OLIST E-Commerce Analytics - Comprehensive Visualization Dashboard

This dashboard presents 10 professional data visualizations analyzing OLIST's e-commerce operations across 100,000+ orders from Brazilian marketplace (Jan 2016 - Sep 2018). Each visualization delivers actionable insights for operational optimization, customer experience enhancement, and strategic decision-making.
